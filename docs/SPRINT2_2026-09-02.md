## 7. VAD endpoint latency experiment — HOST MEASURED, DEVICE NOT MEASURED

Host synthetic (Vad, 64ms callback granularity, `speechChunk` 12k amp, `whisper` 500 amp, `noise` random):

| threshold | short | normal | long | quiet final | whisper | noise | latency | frames |
|-----------|-------|--------|------|-------------|---------|-------|---------|--------|
| 450 | 512ms | 512 | 512 | 512 | 512 | 512 | 8*64 | 8 |
| 500 | 512 | 512 | 512 | 512 | 512 | 512 | 8*64 | 8 |
| 550 | 576 | 576 | 576 | 576 | 576 | 576 | 9*64 | 9 |
| 650 | 704 | 704 | 704 | 704 | 704 | 704 | 11*64 | 11 |

Hesitation: 320ms (5*64) → 0 false splits at all thresholds; 832ms (13*64) → 3 false splits (exceeds 650). Fricative tail `4000 amp` still endpoints, whisper `500 amp` still detected as speech.

**Implication:** 450 and 500 are **identical** due to 64ms quantization (both 8 frames). 550 saves 128ms vs 650, 500 saves 192ms vs 650, but 650 is safest for natural hesitation 400–500ms. No production threshold change before device human speech (DE, whisper, fan, 200–800ms hesitation) — **production stays 650**, **NOT MEASURED** on device.

Frame size 1024 at 16k = 64ms is quantization limit; reducing to 512 (32ms) would halve granularity but double callbacks/CPU — not kept without measurement.
