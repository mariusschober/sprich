First release candidate for Zapstore.

• Offline dictation in English, German, Spanish and French.
• Automatic language detection and an optional Accurate mode.
• Personal vocabulary, spoken editing and delete/undo gestures.
• A compact voice keyboard with a visible keyboard switcher.
• Safer downloads, cursor cancellation and password-field protection.

Android 8.0 or later, ARM64. Download speech files during setup. Online dictation is unavailable in this release. Please review recognized text before sending.
