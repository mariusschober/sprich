Speak instead of typing.

Sprich is a voice keyboard for Android. Dictate in your notes, messages and other text fields, then return to your regular keyboard with one tap.

• Speak English, German, Spanish or French.
• Automatic recognizes the language as you speak.
• Accurate uses a language you choose.
• Add personal vocabulary for names and special terms.
• Swipe to delete, whisper, switch keyboards or hide the bar.
• Optional writing cleanup and API recognition with your own keys.
• No Sprich account, subscription, ads or analytics.

Download the speech files once, then dictate offline. Automatic needs about 219 MB; Accurate is an optional 154 MB download. Your voice stays on your phone when personal APIs are off.

Want writing cleanup or API recognition? Choose a provider, paste your own key and check the connection. Each option stays off until you explicitly enable it. Requests go directly to that provider; Sprich has no server in between. Provider charges and data policies apply. Meta and custom integrations are experimental.

Tap to speak. Pause to insert your words, and tap again to finish. Moving the cursor or leaving the field stops listening and cancels words that have not been inserted. Dictation is unavailable in password and PIN fields.

Requires an ARM64 phone with Android 8.0 or later. The interface is in English. This is a release candidate; speech recognition can make mistakes, so review your text before sending it.
